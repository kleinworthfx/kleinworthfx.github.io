## Purpose

This portfolio organises the evidence for the five assessment categories in the October 2026 Project Presentation 3 brief. The project context is the Digital Payment Solution developed from research into payment challenges faced by informal traders. The submitted research report describes a proposed mobile-based platform with trader registration, unique QR codes or payment links, and a small transaction-fee business model.

The supplied application source archive contains a web prototype called **PayTrader**. Its README describes trader registration/login, QR-code generation, a customer-facing simulated payment flow, dashboard summaries, transaction history, profile management and an admin panel. The supplied code records simulated payments; it does not integrate a real payment gateway. See the [application evidence and source review](application-evidence.md).

## Project context supported by the supplied sources

### Community problem and validation

The Professional Practice 3 report dated 13 March 2026 identifies a problem for informal traders in busy Cape Town trading areas: some customers ask to pay digitally, traders may lose sales when customers only have cash, and handling cash raises safety concerns. The report states that the group conducted eight short interviews and summarises participants' feedback. These are findings reported in the submitted document, not an independently audited survey.

### Proposed solution

The report proposes a phone-based digital payment platform that gives each trader a unique QR code and lets customers complete a payment using a QR code or payment link. It proposes a small transaction fee as a possible revenue model.

### Supplied prototype

The uploaded `DigitalPaymentSolution.zip` contains frontend HTML/CSS/JavaScript and a Node.js/Express backend. Its payment controller explicitly describes payments as simulated, with no real money movement. Do not describe this prototype as a live payment service or claim a gateway integration.
