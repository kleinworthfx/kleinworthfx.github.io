## Scope boundaries in the available project documentation

The documentation describes a browser-based marketplace and states that integrated payment gateways and native Android/iOS apps are outside the initial documented scope. Do not treat these as committed delivery items unless an approved scope change exists.

## Proposed prioritisation rationale

Access control, listing creation and listing discovery are suggested as high priority because they support the core marketplace journey. Messaging, moderation, reviews and collection coordination can be sequenced according to real dependencies, time and approved scope. This is a planning rationale, not a claim about a past team decision.
