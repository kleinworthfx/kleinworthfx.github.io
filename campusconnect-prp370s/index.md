## Project summary

CampusConnect Marketplace is documented as a university-exclusive, browser-based peer-to-peer marketplace for students to buy and sell academic items such as textbooks and calculators. The project documentation identifies the problem as the lack of a safer, more structured campus-specific alternative to informal social-media trading.

The documented requirements include university-email-based access, profiles, item listings with images, search and filtering, buyer–seller messaging, ratings and reviews, administrative moderation, and approved university/library collection points. The project documentation excludes a native Android/iOS app and an integrated payment gateway from the initial scope.
